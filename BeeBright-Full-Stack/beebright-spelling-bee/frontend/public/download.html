🐝 BeeBright on your laptop

A native desktop app with all three 2027 Bee levels. No login or cloud progress. Practice offline using your computer’s voice.

Run this in Windows PowerShell (Windows x64):

irm https://beebright.vercel.app/install.ps1 | iex

After installation, type beebright. Each launch checks for updates; if you’re offline, it opens your installed version. Updates preserve your progress.
